The implementation adds a pure reminder selector and a foreground-only dialog controller. It checks only after authentication plus the first validated deposit snapshot, on snapshot changes, Vietnam date changes, foreground/focus recovery, overview entry and locale changes. It introduces no background delivery mechanism.

## Behavior verified

- D30, D7, D1, D0 and OVERDUE use Vietnam calendar dates and select only the most urgent reached stage.
- Archived, disabled, REDEEMED and ROLLED_OVER deposits do not remind.
- Keys are `<depositId>|<maturesOn>|<stage>`; old-date acknowledgements cannot suppress a changed maturity date.
- Cloud acknowledgement is transactional through UXS-011; local snooze uses a bounded versioned localStorage map and never promises background wakeup.
- Multiple deposits render in one dialog. Plain close does not acknowledge; the next lifecycle check may show the reminder again.
- Firestore `metadata.fromCache` is carried to the UI; cached/offline reminders say they may not be current.
