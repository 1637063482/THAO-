## Owner-Confirmed Rules Input

The project owner provided the current online Firestore Rules semantics during T017:

- Two existing Firebase Auth email accounts are authorized.
- The girlfriend account and project owner account have identical permissions.
- Email comparison is case-insensitive through `request.auth.token.email.lower()`.
- Authorized accounts may `read`, `create`, and `update` `artifacts/{appId}/public/data/ledgers/{ledgerId}`.
- Ledger `delete` is denied.
- All unknown paths are denied.

Real email addresses were intentionally not committed. The repository uses fixture emails ending in `.invalid` for Emulator tests.
