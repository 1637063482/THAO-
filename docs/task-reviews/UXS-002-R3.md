### High - The localized budget heading duplicates the month number

- **File/lines:** `src/js/render.js:94`; `src/locales/vi.js:146`; `src/locales/zh-CN.js:146`.
- **Evidence:** `renderMonthTable(7)` emits a standalone `7` span immediately followed by `t("monthly_budget", { month: 7 })`. The current values are `Ngân sách T{month}` and `{month}月预算`, yielding `7Ngân sách T7` and `77月预算` (with markup spacing), respectively.
- **Impact:** Both locales render an incorrect, duplicated-month budget title.
