## Findings

### High - Six required viewports do not validate the delivered dashboard renderer

- **Files/lines:** `docs/review-evidence/UXS-004.md:97-109`; `docs/review-evidence/assets/UXS-004/viewport-360.png`; `viewport-1920.png`; `TASK_PLAN.md:206,209`; `REVIEW_PLAN.md:121,130`.
- **Evidence:** The R4 evidence says the PNGs are historical R3 artifacts and “are not claimed as live-renderer proof”; it further says screenshot capture used injected synthetic HTML because the auth callback was unavailable. I independently inspected the 360px and 1920px images. They visually show a dashboard, but they cannot establish that `initDashboard()` and `renderDashboard()` produced it, or validate the delivered localization, privacy masking, overflow, and responsive layout at all six widths.
- **Impact:** A mandatory completion and review-plan criterion remains unproven. This cannot be approved merely because unit tests and the build pass.

### Medium - R4's test does not exercise either repaired production refresh path

- **Files/lines:** `src/js/main.js:80-83,174-180`; `tests/unit/dashbaord.test.js:65-70`; `docs/task-reviews/UXS-004-R3.md` Minimum Correction 1.
- **Evidence:** The repaired application paths call `initDashboard()` after the local input debounce and after `switchMonthTab()`. The sole R4 test calls `refreshDashboard()` directly and only asserts that rendered HTML contains `dashboard-root`; it neither edits a legacy entry nor invokes `switchMonthTab()`, and it does not assert that the hero changes while `state` and `pendingUpdates` are preserved.
- **Impact:** The exact regression fixed in R4 is not protected by a behavior-level test. A future change can again leave the dashboard stale without failing this test.

### Medium - Evidence task metadata remains stale and contradicts the submitted R4 state

- **Files/lines:** `docs/review-evidence/UXS-004.md:8-10,102-109`; `TASK_STATUS.md:11-12`.
- **Evidence:** The evidence header records implementation head `72c82b8...` and review round `3`, although its R4 appendix names `311e61c...`; `TASK_STATUS.md` submits review round `4` and that latter head. The evidence also retains R2/R3 claims that its screenshots were captured as a required result, then disclaims them in R4.
- **Impact:** The audit artifact does not identify one unambiguous implementation/review state and cannot serve as the required final evidence record.
