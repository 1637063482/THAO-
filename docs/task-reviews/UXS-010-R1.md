## Blocking findings

### High — Derived MATURING state is reversed and never represents its maturity boundary

- **File:** `src/domain/deposit.ts:47-51`.
- **Evidence:** For every persisted ACTIVE deposit, `today < maturityDate` returns `MATURING`, including dates before its start date. On `today === maturityDate`, it returns `MATURED`. Thus an ordinary active deposit is shown as maturing throughout its term, while the actual maturity boundary has no `MATURING` derived state.
- **Impact:** The required persisted/derived status model is not meaningful and will mislead later UI/workflow code.
- **Minimum correction:** Define and test the explicit date boundaries: before start, during active term, maturity boundary, and after maturity. Keep MATURING strictly derived (never persisted), and retain stable transition errors.

### High — Expected maturity interest and total drift after the maturity date

- **Files:** `src/domain/deposit.ts:53-58, 68-76`.
- **Evidence:** `expectedInterestVnd()` accepts any `asOfDate` after the start and calculates all intervening days without capping/rejecting a date beyond `maturityDate`. `summarizeDeposits()` passes its arbitrary `asOfDate` through and labels `principal + expected` as `expectedMaturityTotalVnd`. Therefore a deposit with a fixed maturity can accrue more expected interest after maturity and its claimed maturity total varies with the viewer's date.
- **Impact:** This violates the required expected-interest/expected-maturity-total semantics and creates a temporal amount drift in a domain model meant to avoid precision drift.
- **Minimum correction:** Define separate accrued-as-of versus expected-at-maturity semantics if both are needed. At minimum, cap or reject post-maturity as-of calculations for the expected maturity value, and add tests before/on/after maturity proving the expected maturity interest and total are stable.
