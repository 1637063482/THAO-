## Findings

### High - Repeated submission produces duplicate legacy writes

- **Files/lines:** `src/js/quick-add.js:82-140`; `index.html:323`; `TASK_PLAN.md:222,226`; `REVIEW_PLAN.md:131`.
- **Evidence:** `submitQuickAdd()` has no local in-flight flag, no `state.isSaving` guard, and does not disable the confirm button. Each invocation immediately reads the same amount, appends it to `existingFormula` at lines 103-109, and writes it into `state.appState.entries`/`pendingUpdates` at lines 109-111. The confirmation button remains enabled, and the new test file has no repeated-click/save-in-progress case.
- **Impact:** A double tap or Enter plus click can turn `=50000` into `=50000+50000`, violating the explicit duplicate-submit completion criterion and producing an unintended expense or income.

### Medium - VND entry accepts fractional persistent facts

- **Files/lines:** `index.html:321`; `src/js/quick-add.js:88-109`.
- **Evidence:** The amount input is `type="number"` with `inputmode="decimal"`. Validation rejects only empty/NaN values, then stores the raw VND value in the legacy formula. For example, VND `1.5` reaches `finalMath = "=1.5"` without integral-VND validation or rounding. CNY conversion has a separate boundary, but the VND route does not.
- **Impact:** This can introduce fractional persisted VND facts, contrary to the VND-integer product contract.

### Medium - Evidence omits the submitted full-gate results

- **Files/lines:** `docs/review-evidence/UXS-005.md:64-66`.
- **Evidence:** The task is already marked `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, but its evidence still says the full test, typecheck, build, and diff gates “will be recorded after the final implementation commit.” The evidence does not give their actual exit codes or outputs.
- **Impact:** The required handoff evidence is incomplete even though the Reviewer independently ran those gates successfully.

## Minimum Correction

1. Add a submit-in-flight guard and disable the confirm control before invoking the legacy write/sync path. Ensure any success/failure completion restores the control appropriately, without clearing or closing on failure. Add tests for double click and Enter-plus-click proving exactly one legacy formula/pending update is written.
2. Enforce integral positive VND at the Quick Add boundary (while preserving the existing CNY-to-VND conversion behavior), with tests for decimal/zero/negative rejection.
3. Update UXS-005 evidence with the final implementation SHA and actual full-gate commands, exit codes, and key outputs before returning to `READY_FOR_REVIEW`.
